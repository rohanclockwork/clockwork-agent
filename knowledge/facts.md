# Clockwork facts (team-edited; update this file when anything changes)

## Clockwork Daily Toothpaste (Peppermint, 100 g)
- 10% nanoXIM·CarePaste: nano-hydroxyapatite made by FLUIDINOVA in Portugal. Rod-shaped (95.8% of particles at aspect ratio below 3), under 100 nm (median length 27.6 nm), uncoated. EU REACH registration 01-2119490075-38-0021. The rod-shaped form is the one Europe's Scientific Committee on Consumer Safety (SCCS) assessed for toothpaste (SCCS/1648/22, 2023).
- 10% xylitol, derived from birch.
- Fluoride free, SLS free, paraben free, no artificial dyes, no synthetic sweeteners. Steam-distilled natural flavours.
- Ingredients (label order): Glycerin, Hydrated Silica, Calcium Carbonate, Hydroxyapatite (nano), Xylitol, Aqua, Disodium Cocoyl Glutamate, Flavour, Stevia Rebaudiana Leaf Extract, Guar Gum, Potassium Chloride, Cocos Nucifera (Coconut) Oil, Sodium Bicarbonate.
- Made in India by Prevest DenPro under licence. Marketed by Clockwork Rituals Private Limited.
- Flavours coming soon: Bold Cinnamon Mint, Spearmint.
- Use: a pea-sized amount, twice a day, two minutes. Spit, don't rinse.

## Evidence
- Clinical trials of hydroxyapatite toothpastes (not Clockwork) show "as good as" fluoride at preventing new decay in the products tested (Paszynska et al., 2023: 194 adults, 18 months, non-inferior to 1,450 ppm fluoride). None tested Clockwork. Studies on Clockwork are under way at IDST, India; no results yet.
